# Jinku Guo

Mathematics, physics and computation from minimal unbiased change.

Northwestern Polytechnical University · [ORCID](https://orcid.org/0009-0000-6600-6171) · [Email](mailto:guojk@nwpu.edu.cn)

[Research portal](https://bookwormseagull1980.github.io/Book-and-Articles/) · [Papers](https://bookwormseagull1980.github.io/Book-and-Articles/#papers) · [Research discussions](https://github.com/bookwormseagull1980/Book-and-Articles/discussions)

My research programme follows a common generating source into mathematical
objects, physical response and finite certified computation. The central
question is what a construction produces, and which information must remain
available when the result is observed at finite resolution.

## Research directions

- **Generated arithmetic and histories.** Native arithmetic on a correlation
  spectrum, finite expression fibres, conditional readout and history-assembly
  dynamics. Retained history differences can affect subsequent value response.
- **Prime decomposition and operators.** Prime irreducibility, cotangent
  directions, quantitative Hodge projection, occupation traces and signed
  Gaussian scale response.
- **Gauge structure and Yang-Mills spectral gaps.** Generated gauge-matter
  response, the nonlinear curvature action, the complete original-field
  Hessian and its attained positive spectral bottom.
- **Gravity and cosmic structure.** Source-resolved long-range exchange,
  coarse-graining with memory and finite-window cosmological dynamics.
- **Certified computation and complexity.** Generated function classes,
  rational trajectory records, residual certificates, polynomial bit-cost
  theorems and the uniform-generation connection to P vs NP.
